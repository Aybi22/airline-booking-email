Airline Booking Confirmation Email — Case Study

Designing a responsive booking confirmation and check-in email experience for airline passengers with a focus on clarity, accessibility and consistency.

Project overview

Project: Airline Email design

Brief

Design two responsive transactional emails for an airline:

  1. Booking confirmation Email
  2. Check-in reminder

These emails should communicate important travel information while maintaining a trustworthy and professional experience.

Goals

  • Improve readability of airline communications
  • Build a responsive Email for Desktop and mobile
  • Follow accessibility best practices
  • Build reusable components through design system

design challenge

Airline confirmation and check-in emails play a critical role in the passenger journey. They must present essential travel information clearly while reducing stress and helping passengers prepare for their trip. The challenge was to design two responsive transactional emails that communicate booking details and check-in instructions in a clean, accessible, and easy-to-scan format across desktop and mobile devices.

design process

I followed a structured design process to transform the airline email requirements into a clear and responsive user experience. The process included wireframing, visual design and building reusable design systems.

Wireframes were created to define the structure of the content hierarchy of the emails before applying styles. the focus was on organising information and ensuring a smooth user journey across mobile and desktop.

Final outcome

The final solution consists of two responsive transactional email designs: a booking confirmation email and a check-in reminder email. Both emails use a consistent visual language, reusable components, and a clear information hierarchy to help passengers quickly access essential travel information on both desktop and mobile devices.

Design System

key elements

A reusable design system was created to ensure consistency across the booking confirmation and check-in emails. It defines typography, colour palette, spacing, buttons, icons, and reusable components, making the designs easier to maintain and scale.

  • Typography – Established a clear text hierarchy for headings, subheadings, labels, and body content.
  • Colour Palette – Defined primary and neutral colours to reinforce the airline's visual identity.
  • Spacing – Created consistent spacing rules to improve readability and alignment
  • UI Elements – Standardised buttons and icons for a cohesive user experience
  • Reusable Components – Built shared components such as the header, footer, hero section, flight summary, and booking details to support consistency across multiple email templates.

reflection

This project helped me gain a deeper understanding of the end-to-end design process, from initial concept and wireframing to high-fidelity designs and a reusable design system. Throught the project, I developed my skills in creating reusable components and maintaining UI elements in Figma. I also learned how to use auto layout effectively to build flexible , scalable and well-structured interfaces thay can be adapted across different email templates and screen sizes. this experience reinforced the importance of consisitency, accessibility and responsive design when creating user-centered digital experiences
